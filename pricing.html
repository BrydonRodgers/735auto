Pricing Questions
Are these prices final?
Yes — the prices on this page are our confirmed rates. No hidden fees or surprises on the day. Commercial fleet contracts are the only pricing negotiated case-by-case.
How do I book at these prices?
Message us on WhatsApp with your vehicle type and any add-ons you want (or fleet size and frequency for commercial) and we'll confirm your slot.
Does the B-BBEE Level 1 status affect pricing for commercial clients?
Our rates are competitive on their own — the B-BBEE Level 1, 100% Black-owned status is a separate, genuine procurement scoring advantage under PPPFA for IDZ tenants, on top of the service itself.
