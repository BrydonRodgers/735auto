Pricing Questions
Why can't I just see a price list?
735 Auto's final rates are still being confirmed internally. We'd rather quote you accurately on WhatsApp than publish a number that changes before you book.
How fast will I get a quote?
Message us your vehicle type and tier (or fleet size and frequency for commercial) on WhatsApp — we'll respond with a price and available slots.
Does the B-BBEE Level 1 status affect pricing for commercial clients?
Our rates are competitive on their own — the B-BBEE Level 1, 100% Black-owned status is a separate, genuine procurement scoring advantage under PPPFA for IDZ tenants, on top of the service itself.
